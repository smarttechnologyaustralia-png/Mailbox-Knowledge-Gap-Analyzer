# Smart Technology brand foundation

## Positioning

Smart Technology turns operational data into clear, practical decisions.

**Brand line:** Clear insight. Better service.

## Visual identity

- Deep navy `#0C223D` — trust, governance and enterprise credibility
- Action teal `#147D76` — intelligence, progress and positive action
- Signal teal `#43C7B5` — highlights and the ST monogram
- Cloud `#F6F8FC` — primary application background
- Slate `#5D6F81` — supporting text

Use the supplied `assets/smart-technology-mark.svg` without changing its
proportions or colours. Maintain clear space equal to approximately one quarter
of the mark's width.

## Typography and tone

Use a modern system sans-serif with strong hierarchy and restrained weight.
Copy should be direct, evidence-led and calm. Avoid exaggerated claims,
unexplained technical language and playful visual devices.
