Questions

The things people ask before uploading.

Do you get access to our email?

No. You export a file and upload it. We never connect to your mail account and never see your live inbox.

What happens to the data?

Personal information — names, email addresses, phone numbers and Australian identifiers including TFNs, Medicare numbers and ABNs — is redacted before any content is analysed. Nothing is used to train AI models. For paid work, exports and working copies are deleted within fourteen days, confirmed in writing on request.

Is the free version limited?

The free scan gives you the cost picture and the top question themes. The paid tiers add verified analysis of each email and drafted answer content. You'll see exactly what's included before paying anything.
