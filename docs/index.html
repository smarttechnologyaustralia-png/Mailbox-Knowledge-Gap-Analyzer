Our perspective
Sometimes the right advice is: don't buy AI.

Much of what presents as a technology problem is a process problem. If a redesigned form, a clearer handoff or a checklist resolves it, that is what we will recommend — because our model depends on you returning with the next problem, not regretting this one. Fifteen years of process improvement taught us that the tool is the last decision, not the first.

The firm

Boutique by design. Senior on every engagement.

Smart Technology Australia is led by Abhishek Arora — a process improvement consultant with more than fifteen years inside large Australian organisations across telecommunications, energy, FMCG and medical research.

The premise of the firm is simple: the person who analyses your problem is the person who designs and delivers the fix. No leverage model, no handoffs to juniors, no forty-page decks where four pages of plain language will do.

What has changed is what can now be built. Answer systems, document analysers, assurance registers and automated reporting — work that once required a development team — can be delivered in weeks at a fraction of the historical cost. We pair traditional process analysis with that capability, end to end.

Every deliverable is written in plain language. If a report needs a glossary, we've failed.
