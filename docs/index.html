The method

Simple, repeatable, evidence-led — and the same on every engagement.

The hardest problems organisations face are rarely just process problems. They are system problems seen as process problems. So we look across the whole system first, then move in four disciplined steps.

01

Discover

Understand how the work really happens — workshops, interviews, process walk-throughs, voice of the user.

02

Analyse

Use data and evidence to establish what's working, what isn't and why — time & motion studies, root-cause analysis, mapping.

03

Design

Co-create practical, human-centred solutions — future-state design, a North Star with an MVP pathway, prioritisation.

04

Deliver & improve

Test, implement, embed — pilot with real users, measure outcomes, iterate. This is where most engagements stop. Ours don't.

Before you ask

The questions we get in the first call.

We're small. Is this only for big organisations?

No. The opening assessment is deliberately small enough for a five-person business to justify. The one honest qualifier: the inbox audit guarantee assumes a shared mailbox handling roughly 400+ emails a month — below that the numbers won't justify the work, and we'll say so rather than take the fee.

What does it cost?

Every engagement is fixed-fee and fixed-scope, quoted in a short written proposal once we understand the problem — because a number quoted before the problem is understood is either a guess or a hedge. Entry assessments are priced so a small business can start without a business case; larger builds and transformation work are scoped and quoted properly. Ask, and you'll have a written figure quickly.

What happens to our data?

For inbox work you provide an export, not access to your live mail. Personal information — names, emails, phone numbers and Australian identifiers including TFNs, Medicare and ABNs — is redacted automatically before any content is analysed. Your data is never used to train AI models, and we delete exports and working copies within fourteen days of delivery, confirmed in writing on request. All of this is in the engagement letter, not just on this page.

What if the assessment says we shouldn't do anything?

Then that's the deliverable, and it's a good one — you've avoided a much larger bill. If the assessment doesn't identify at least five recoverable hours a week, the fee is refunded in full.

Do we own what you build?

Yes, outright, documented and handed over. You own your data and the delivered solution; we retain only our own methods, templates and tooling. No lock-in, no ongoing licence required to keep using what you paid for.

Can you work alongside our existing team or vendor?

Regularly. Much of the work is defining requirements clearly enough for an internal team or external vendor to deliver well — and then leading the testing that proves they did. We're just as comfortable handing designs to your developers as building it ourselves.

How quickly can you start?

Assessments usually begin within a week of engagement and deliver five business days later. Sprints and builds are scheduled around a small number of concurrent clients — deliberately, so senior attention stays genuine.
