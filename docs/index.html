We're small. Is this only for big organisations?

No. The opening assessment is deliberately small enough for a five-person business to justify. One honest qualifier: the inbox diagnostic assumes a shared mailbox handling roughly 400+ emails a month — below that, the numbers won't justify the work, and we'll say so rather than take the fee.

What does it cost?

Fixed fee, fixed scope, quoted in a short written proposal once we understand the problem — because a number quoted before that is either a guess or a hedge. Entry assessments are priced so a small business can start without a business case. Ask, and you'll have a written figure quickly.

What happens to our data?

For inbox work you provide an export, not access to your live mail. Names, email addresses, phone numbers and Australian identifiers including TFNs, Medicare numbers and ABNs are redacted automatically before any content is analysed. Nothing is used to train AI models, and exports are deleted within fourteen days of delivery, confirmed in writing on request. This is in the engagement letter, not just on this page.

What if the answer is that we shouldn't do anything?

Then that's the deliverable, and it's a good one — you've avoided a much larger bill. If an inbox assessment doesn't identify at least five recoverable hours a week, the fee is refunded in full.

Can you work with our existing team or vendor?

Regularly. Much of the work is defining requirements clearly enough for an internal team or external vendor to deliver well, then leading the testing that proves they did. We're as comfortable handing designs to your developers as building it ourselves.

Tell us what's broken

Three or four sentences is plenty — what the work is, how often it happens, and who it frustrates. No brief, no NDA needed to describe a problem in general terms. You'll hear back directly, usually the same day, with one of three things.

  1. Here's what I think is actually going on — and what I'd look at first, in plain language.
  2. This is a fit. Here's the proposal — scope, deliverables, timeline and fixed fee on one page.
  3. This isn't worth paying us for — with what to try instead, or who's better suited. Saying so is cheaper for both of us than a bad engagement.
