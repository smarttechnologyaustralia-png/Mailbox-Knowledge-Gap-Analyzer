Three engagements from fifteen years inside large Australian organisations. Same method, different industries.

EnergyAustralia
30%

fewer exceptions in customer onboarding

Challenge

High exception rates in customer onboarding and smart metering were causing delays and poor first-bill outcomes for customers.

What we did

Conducted time and motion studies and analysed operational data to identify bottlenecks and root causes. Strengthened validation and business rules, then redesigned the workflow around them.

Outcome

Roughly a 30% reduction in exception handling, with improved first-bill outcomes and a better customer experience.

Asahi Beverages
15%

less manual HR intervention

Challenge

Three payroll systems after multiple acquisitions led to inconsistent workforce processes and heavy manual effort across the business.

What we did

Facilitated discovery across HR, payroll, operations and technology. Mapped the as-is processes, separated policy issues from system issues, and designed a consolidated to-be model on SAP SuccessFactors.

Outcome

Simplified and standardised processes, reducing manual HR intervention by around 15%.

NBN
National

field-operations remediation capability

Challenge

Fragmented hand-offs and inconsistent practices across national field operations were affecting technician performance and customer outcomes.

What we did

As capability and product owner, redesigned quality management and remediation processes with field teams and owned the improvement backlog. Designed clean, intuitive UI/UX with the UX team, aligned to NBN ways of working, and tested with frontline users before release.

Outcome

A national remediation capability that improved workforce utilisation and service outcomes.
